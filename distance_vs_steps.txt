Sensor Robot - Distance to Steps Calculation
=============================================

Formula:
  wheel radius        = 3 cm
  wheel circumference = 2 * pi * radius = 18.8496 cm  (one full 200-step revolution)
  steps per cm         = 200 / circumference = 10.6103 steps/cm
  steps(distance_cm)   = round(distance_cm * steps_per_cm)

Results:
  For 10cm the robot showed 106 steps.
  For 20cm the robot showed 212 steps.
  For 30cm the robot showed 318 steps.
  For 50cm the robot showed 531 steps.
  For 100cm the robot showed 1061 steps.
